Pre-Built Bio (Ready to Use)

Queens-born and Phoenix-raised, Murkemz is the CEO of Plug City Records and one of the most credentialed independent MCs in the Southwest. A purist in the vein of the legends who came before him, Murkemz seamlessly switches flows and tempos — described by Phoenix New Times as moving "like gears in an Italian exotic." His 2025 collaboration with Inspectah Deck (Wu-Tang Clan) on "Survive" has surpassed 244,000 streams, while the Hadouken RMX featuring Chris Webby stands at 541,000 streams — his biggest to date. 2026 opened with a Freeway (Roc-A-Fella Records) collaboration and the breakout anthem "PHX", now trending nationally. Endorsed by Ice-T at the Grammy Awards, featured on Hot 97 and Sway's Universe, and named Phoenix New Times Best Hip-Hop Artist 2025 — Murkemz is building the independent label infrastructure for the next generation of artists, managed by Bryce Breeze.
